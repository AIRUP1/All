## Rules
- Skills are advice, not authority. Verify any platform spec marked VERIFY before stating it.
- Outreach compliance: before any cold email, SMS or call list work, check CAN-SPAM, TCPA and state rules, honor opt-outs, and use only consented or properly sourced contacts. Do not build tools that harvest personal data from people-search sites.
- Never put credentials in files, pages or skills. Use environment variables or a secret store.
